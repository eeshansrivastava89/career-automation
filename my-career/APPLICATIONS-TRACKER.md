# Applications Tracker

Your job search pipeline. Every application, every status, every outcome.

> Your job search preferences (locations, roles, comp, visa) live in **PROFILE.md**. The job scorer reads both files when evaluating fit.

---

## How the automation works

1. **You find a job** — paste URL or JD text into your AI agent
2. **Agent scores it** — match score across 5 dimensions + company research + your preferences from PROFILE.md
3. **You decide** — apply or skip based on the recommendation
4. **Agent tailors resume** — from your corpus, for this specific role
5. **Agent generates prep** — if you get an interview
6. **You update** — track outcomes and learn from every round
