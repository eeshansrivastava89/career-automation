# Sample Job Description for Testing

## Senior Manager, Growth Analytics — Trailhead Outdoors

**Company:** Trailhead Outdoors
**Location:** Seattle, WA (hybrid 3 days/week)
**Comp:** $175K–$220K base + equity
**Visa:** H1B sponsorship available

---

### About the Role

Trailhead Outdoors is looking for a Senior Manager of Growth Analytics to lead our analytics function across customer acquisition, lifecycle marketing, and product growth. You will manage a team of 4-6 analysts and partner closely with Product, Marketing, and Engineering to define KPIs, design experiments, build predictive models, and deliver insights that directly impact business decisions.

This role reports to the VP of Data & Analytics and is a key leadership position in a growing 20-person data organization.

### What You'll Do

- Define and own the KPI framework for growth — acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue
- Lead experimentation across marketing campaigns, product features, and lifecycle programs
- Build predictive models for customer LTV, churn, and segmentation
- Manage and mentor a team of 4-6 analysts; grow the team over the next year
- Partner with Marketing leaders on budget allocation decisions across paid channels
- Present analysis and recommendations to VP and C-suite stakeholders
- Design and implement measurement approaches for incrementality and attribution
- Build self-serve dashboards and reporting tools for the growth organization

### Required Qualifications

- 7+ years in analytics or data science, with 3+ years managing teams
- Strong SQL skills — you can write complex queries from scratch under time pressure
- Experience with experimentation (A/B tests, multivariate tests, geo-lift or holdout designs)
- Experience with marketing analytics (attribution, CAC/LTV, ROAS optimization, channel analysis)
- Proficiency in Python or R for analysis and modeling
- Experience presenting to senior leadership with clear, actionable recommendations
- Experience building KPI frameworks and driving data-informed decision making

### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in e-commerce, direct-to-consumer, or subscription businesses
- Familiarity with causal inference methods (CUPED, DiD, synthetic control)
- Experience with ML models for customer segmentation or propensity scoring
- Experience scaling analytics teams (hiring, mentoring, building processes)
- Familiarity with tools like Looker, Tableau, Amplitude, or similar

### About Trailhead Outdoors

Trailhead Outdoors is a direct-to-consumer outdoor gear and apparel company based in Seattle. Founded in 2018, we've grown to $400M in annual revenue with 2M+ active customers. Our growth has been powered by a strong brand, community-driven marketing, and a data-informed approach to product and marketing decisions. We're profitable and recently closed a $200M Series E round to accelerate growth in new categories and international markets.
